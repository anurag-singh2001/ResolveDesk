# Customer guide

ResolveDesk is where you see your orders and get help when something goes wrong with one. You
tell us what happened, we check the order's records, and a person approves any refund before it
is paid.

## Signing in

1. Open the app. You see the **Sign in** page.
2. Under **Business**, choose the restaurant or café you ordered from.
3. Enter the **email** and **password** for your account, then click **Sign in**.

If you see **"Wrong email or password"**, check both and try again. After five wrong tries the
app says **"Too many attempts, try again in a few minutes"**. Wait about 15 minutes before trying
again.

You stay signed in while this browser tab is open. If you are away for a long time, or close the
tab, you will see **"Your session has ended"**. Just sign in again. Click **Sign out** (top right)
when you are done on a shared computer.

## My orders

**My orders** lists your recent orders as cards. Each card shows:

- the **order number** (for example `O0001234`),
- the **brand and outlet** that made it,
- the **date and time** you placed it,
- the **total** in rupees,
- the order's **status** (for example *Delivered*).

Click a card to open the order. The order page shows:

- every **item**, with how many you **ordered** and how many the records say you **received**. A
  received number in red means fewer arrived than you ordered;
- the time the order was **promised by** and the time it was **delivered**.

## Reporting a problem

1. Open the order from **My orders**.
2. Click **Report a problem**.
3. Under **What went wrong?**, pick the closest match, such as *Late delivery* or *Missing items*.
   If none fits, or you are not sure, pick **Not sure — just describe it** (when shown) and our
   assistant will work out the category from your message.
4. For some problems (for example a wrong item) a **How would you like this settled?** list appears,
   such as *Voucher* or *Ask for a refund*. Each choice shows what you would get, worked out for your order, and
   how long it usually takes: "Usually instant" or "After a team member approves it". Pick one. If only
   one remedy is possible, no list is shown. Your choice never changes the limits: the amount is always
   set by our rules, and a refund is a request that a team member decides on. If the option you picked
   cannot be given any more, we use another that is allowed and note it. If you did not pick a category yet
   (*Not sure*), the same list may appear on the case page while we are still looking at your report.
5. Under **Tell us what happened**, describe the problem in your own words.
6. Click **Submit report**.

You go straight to the **case page** for your report.

**Privacy requests.** To ask about your personal data, pick **Privacy request** as the category. It is
never handled by the assistant: it goes straight to a member of our team, and the case page names our
**Privacy contact** and the date by which we aim to respond. That date is our own internal target, not a
legal deadline. The Privacy contact is also linked in the footer of every page and on the **Help** page.

## My cases and the case page

**My cases** lists every problem you have reported, newest first, with its current stage. Click
one to open it.

The case page shows:

- a **progress bar** (the stages below), with the current stage highlighted;
- a **message** telling you where things stand right now;
- the **proposed remedy**, if there is one: a refund or a voucher, with the amount;
- a **timeline** of what has happened so far, with times.

While the case is *Investigating* or *Processing*, the page updates by itself every few seconds.
If it takes more than two minutes, click **Refresh** to check again.

If the case is **Awaiting info**, we have asked you a question and a **Your reply** box appears on
the case page. Type your answer and press **Send reply**; the case carries on by itself and the
timeline shows "You replied".

### What each stage means

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | We have your report. |
| **Investigating** | We are checking your order's records: items, times and payment. |
| **Waiting for your reply** | We need one more detail from you. The message says what. |
| **Awaiting approval** | We have proposed a remedy and a member of staff is reviewing it. |
| **Processing** | It was approved (by staff, or automatically for a small voucher) and is being carried out, for example the refund has been sent to the payment provider. |
| **Confirmed** | Done. For a refund, the payment provider has confirmed it; for a voucher, it has been issued. |
| **Declined** | Staff reviewed it and no remedy was approved. The message explains. |
| **With our team** | A person is handling your case directly. |
| **Closed** | The case is finished. |

## What happens after you report

1. Our assistant reads your message and checks the facts on record for **your** order only.
2. It looks up your business's policy and proposes a remedy (for example a refund for a missing
   item, or a voucher). The amount comes from the business's rules, not from the assistant.
3. **A person approves every refund.** Some small vouchers may be approved automatically.
4. When a refund is approved, it is sent to the payment provider. Your case says **Confirmed**
   only after the provider confirms the money has moved.

## What the assistant does and doesn't do

- It **does** check your order's real records and propose a fair remedy under the policy.
- It **does** ask you a short question if something is unclear.
- It **does not** pay anything itself. People approve refunds.
- It **never promises money** before the payment provider confirms it. If the case says
  *Processing*, the refund is on its way but not yet confirmed.
- It only ever looks at **your** orders.

## Our policies

Open **Our policies** in the menu to read the rules we follow. Each policy shows the date it came into force, and each section has its own link, so you can share or bookmark the exact part you mean. The text is shown exactly as written. You only see your own business's policies.

Above the policies, **Rules in plain words** says what you get for each kind of problem, for example a voucher up to the value of your order and at most a stated amount, or a refund of up to a share of your order after a team member approves it. The figures come straight from your business's current rules, so they change when the rules change, and the date they have been in force is shown at the top. It also says when a team member decides instead of the system.

## Privacy and data

Open **Privacy & data** in the menu.

- **Offers and promotions.** Help with an order never needs your permission for offers. If you
  want offers on WhatsApp, turn the switch on; to stop them, turn it off. One click either way,
  and it applies to the next message we would send. **History of your choices** lists each
  change with its date.
- **Download my data.** Saves a file with your details, orders, cases, your choices above, and the
  companies that process data for us (such as the AI providers and the database host).
- **Erase my data.** Click **Request erasure**. A person reviews it, and then your name, contact
  details and message text are removed. Records of payments and refunds are kept. The page shows
  the request's status: **Waiting for review**, **Completed**, or **Deferred** with the reason
  (for example a case or refund of yours is still open, or we must keep records for one year after
  your last activity). A deferred request is not dropped; ask the business to review it again
  once the reason has passed.

## Language for our messages

We reply in the language you write in. You do not choose a setting.

- If you write in **English**, we reply in English. If you write in **Hinglish** (Hindi in English
  letters), we reply in Hinglish. If you write in **हिन्दी** (Devanagari), we reply in Hindi.
- The language is set from your first message about a report and stays the same for that report.
  It changes only if you write in Devanagari, or send two longer messages in a different language.
  Very short replies such as "haan", "yes" or "ok", and messages that are only an order number or
  an emoji, never change it.
- Only our messages about your reports change. The buttons, menus and headings of this app stay
  in English.
- Amounts look the same in every language (for example `₹1,00,000.00`), and dates use a month
  name (for example `27 Oct 2026`), never numbers like `10/11`.
- The messages are fixed, pre-written sentences, not generated text. The Hindi and Hinglish
  versions say exactly what the English ones say, and keep *waiting for approval*, *being
  processed*, *done*, *failed* and *needs a check* clearly different. If a sentence has no
  translation, you see it in English.
