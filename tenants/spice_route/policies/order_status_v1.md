# Order status questions (v1)

## 1. Where is my order
### 1.1 Verified status only
Share the status our kitchen and delivery records show: received, preparing, out for delivery or delivered, with the promised time. Never guess an arrival time that the records don't support.

### 1.2 No compensation for asking
A status question on its own carries no compensation. If the order then arrives late, the late delivery policy applies.

## 2. Cancellations
### 2.1 Before preparation
An order cancelled before the kitchen starts preparing it is refunded in full to the original payment method.

### 2.2 After preparation
An order cancelled after preparation has started needs a manager's decision.
