# Payment issues (v1)

## 1. Charged twice or charged for a failed order
### 1.1 Remedy
A team member checks the charge with the payment provider before anything is promised. When the provider confirms a duplicate charge, or a charge for an order that was never prepared, the extra amount is refunded once to the original payment method.

### 1.2 What we tell the customer
Until the provider has confirmed, say only that the payment is being checked. Never tell a customer that money has been returned before the provider confirms the refund.

### 1.3 Debited but no order confirmation
When money left the customer's account but the payment failed or the order was never confirmed, the bank reverses the debit within the timeline set per RBI turnaround-time rules. Tell the customer only what our payment provider has confirmed. Never promise the bank's compensation for a delayed reversal: that is between the customer and their bank.

## 2. Refund status questions
### 2.1 Reporting progress
Report the refund's state exactly as our records show it: requested, approved, processing, completed or failed. Once our payment provider confirms a refund is completed, the customer's bank credits it within the timeline set per RBI turnaround-time rules. Say so when the refund is completed but the customer can't see it yet; don't quote a number of days as a promise.

## 3. Payment method
### 3.1 Original method only
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We never refund to a different account, card or UPI ID, even when the customer asks.
