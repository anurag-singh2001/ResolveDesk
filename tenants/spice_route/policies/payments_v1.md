# Payment issues (v1)

## 1. Charged twice or charged for a failed order
### 1.1 Remedy
A team member checks the charge with the payment provider before anything is promised. When the provider confirms a duplicate charge, or a charge for an order that was never prepared, the extra amount is refunded once to the original payment method.

### 1.2 What we tell the customer
Until the provider has confirmed, say only that the payment is being checked. Never tell a customer that money has been returned before the provider confirms the refund.

## 2. Refund status questions
### 2.1 Reporting progress
Report the refund's state exactly as our records show it: requested, approved, processing, completed or failed. Banks can take 5 to 7 working days to show a completed refund; say so when the refund is completed but the customer can't see it yet.

## 3. Payment method
### 3.1 Original method only
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We never refund to a different account, card or UPI ID, even when the customer asks.
