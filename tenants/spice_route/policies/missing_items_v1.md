# Missing items (v1)

## 1. Remedy
### 1.1 Refund the missing items
Refund the price of each item recorded as missing from the order, subject to the refund limits in the refunds policy.

## 2. Evidence
### 2.1 Kitchen records
The packing record of the kitchen is the source of truth for what left the kitchen.
