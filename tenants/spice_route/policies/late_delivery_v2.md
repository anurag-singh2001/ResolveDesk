# Late delivery policy (v2)

## 1. Scope
Applies to delivery orders from all Spice Route Foods brands placed before 1 September 2026.

## 2. Remedies
### 2.1 Over 60 minutes late
If an order arrives more than 60 minutes after the promised time, offer a voucher for a future order. Refunds are not offered for lateness under this version.

### 2.2 Under 60 minutes late
Apologise and log the delay for the kitchen team.
