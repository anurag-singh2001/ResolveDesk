# Wrong item delivered (v1)

## 1. Remedy
### 1.1 Voucher for the wrong item
When a customer receives a dish they didn't order, offer a voucher for a future order, up to the tenant's voucher limit.

### 1.2 Wrong order entirely
When the whole order belongs to someone else, a manager decides between a voucher and a refund of the order.

## 2. Evidence
### 2.1 Order records
Check the items recorded on the order before offering a remedy.
