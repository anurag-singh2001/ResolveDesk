# Refunds (v2)

## 1. General rules
### 1.1 Approval
Every refund is approved by a person. Refunds above ₹500 need the owner's approval.

### 1.2 Limits
A single refund is at most 50% of the order value. All refunds on one order together never exceed what was paid.

## 2. Method
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We tell the customer only once the payment provider confirms the refund.
