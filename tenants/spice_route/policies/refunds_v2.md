# Refunds (v2)

## 1. General rules
### 1.1 Approval
Every refund is approved by a person. Refunds above ₹500 need the owner's approval.

### 1.2 Limits
A single refund is at most 50% of the order value. All refunds on one order together never exceed what was paid.

## 2. Method
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We tell the customer only once the payment provider confirms the refund.

## 3. Orders placed through a delivery app
### 3.1 The delivery app refunds
For an order placed through a third-party delivery app (Swiggy, Zomato), that app collected the payment and handles refunds under its own rules. We don't refund those orders ourselves: we offer a voucher for a future order, or pass the case to a person.
