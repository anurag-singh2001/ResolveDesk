# Late delivery policy (v4)

## 1. Scope
Applies to delivery orders from all Spice Route Foods brands placed on or after 26 October 2026, through our own app or website. Lateness is measured in whole minutes from the promised delivery time shown to the customer, using our delivery records.

## 2. Remedies
### 2.1 60 minutes late or more
Refund 50% of the order value to the original payment method. The refund needs approval by a manager and never exceeds what the customer paid.

### 2.2 45 to 59 minutes late
Refund 25% of the order value to the original payment method. The refund needs approval by a manager and never exceeds what the customer paid.

### 2.3 20 to 44 minutes late
Offer a voucher for a future order, up to the tenant's voucher limit.

### 2.4 Under 20 minutes late
Apologise. No compensation.

## 3. Exclusions
### 3.1 Customer-caused delays
No remedy when our delivery record shows the delay was caused by a wrong address or the customer being unreachable. The customer's own account is investigated, but on its own it doesn't change the record.

### 3.2 Orders placed through a delivery app
For orders placed through a third-party delivery app (Swiggy, Zomato), that app handles refunds under its own rules. We don't refund those orders ourselves; we offer a voucher for a future order or pass the case to a person.
