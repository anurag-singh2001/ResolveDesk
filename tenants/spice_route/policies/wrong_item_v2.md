# Wrong item delivered (v2)

## 1. Remedy
### 1.1 Voucher for the wrong item
When a customer receives a dish they didn't order, we offer a voucher for a future order, up to the tenant's voucher limit. This is the default.

### 1.2 Refund on request
The customer may ask for a refund instead of the voucher. A team member decides every refund request; it is never automatic. A refund is at most 50% of the order value, as set out in the refunds policy (refunds_v2 section 1.2), and never more than was paid.

### 1.3 Wrong order entirely
When the whole order belongs to someone else, a manager decides between a voucher and a refund, within the same limit.

### 1.4 Orders placed through a delivery app
For an order placed through a third-party delivery app (Swiggy, Zomato), that app handles refunds under its own rules. We don't refund those orders ourselves: we offer a voucher for a future order, or pass the case to a person.

## 2. Evidence
### 2.1 Order records
Check the items recorded on the order before offering a remedy.
