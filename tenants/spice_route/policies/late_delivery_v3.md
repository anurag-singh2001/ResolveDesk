# Late delivery policy (v3)

## 1. Scope
Applies to delivery orders from all Spice Route Foods brands placed on or after 1 September 2026. Lateness is measured from the promised delivery time shown to the customer.

## 2. Remedies
### 2.1 More than 45 minutes late
Refund 50% of the order value to the original payment method. The refund needs approval by a manager and never exceeds what the customer paid.

### 2.2 20 to 45 minutes late
Offer a voucher for a future order, up to the tenant's voucher limit.

### 2.3 Under 20 minutes late
Apologise. No compensation.

## 3. Exclusions
### 3.1 Customer-caused delays
No remedy when the delay was caused by a wrong address or the customer being unreachable.
