# Food quality complaints (v1)

## 1. Cold or spoiled food
### 1.1 Remedy
Offer a voucher for the affected item. Ask for a photo when the complaint is about spoiled food.

## 2. Allergens
### 2.1 Escalation
Any allergen complaint is escalated to the owner immediately. No automatic remedy.
