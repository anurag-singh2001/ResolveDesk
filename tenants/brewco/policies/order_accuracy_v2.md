# Order accuracy (v2)

## 1. Wrong drink or item
### 1.1 Remedy
Remake or voucher first: when the guest is still at the outlet, offer a remake. Otherwise offer a voucher for the item on the guest's next visit, up to the tenant's voucher limit. A refund comes second, only when a manager decides a remake or voucher doesn't fit.

## 2. Missing item on a pickup order
### 2.1 Remedy
Refund the price of the missing item to the original payment method.
