# Order accuracy (v2)

## 1. Wrong drink or item
### 1.1 Remedy
Offer a voucher for the item on the guest's next visit. A remake is offered when the guest is still at the outlet.

## 2. Missing item on a pickup order
### 2.1 Remedy
Refund the price of the missing item to the original payment method.
