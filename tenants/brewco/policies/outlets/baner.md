# Baner outlet refunds (v1)

## 1. Approval
### 1.1 Lower escalation limit
At the Baner outlet, refunds above ₹300 need the owner's approval. Up to ₹300 the outlet manager approves.

## 2. Limits
### 2.1 Order limit
Refunds on one order never exceed what the guest paid.
