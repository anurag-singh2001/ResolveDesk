# Payment issues (v1)

## 1. Charged twice or charged for a failed order
### 1.1 Remedy
A team member checks the charge with the payment provider before anything is promised. When the provider confirms a duplicate charge, or a charge for an order that was never made, the extra amount is refunded once to the original payment method.

### 1.2 What we tell the guest
Until the provider has confirmed, say only that the payment is being checked. Never tell a guest that money has been returned before the provider confirms the refund.

### 1.3 Debited but no order confirmation
When money left the guest's account but the payment failed or the order was never confirmed, the bank reverses the debit within the timeline set per RBI turnaround-time rules. Tell the guest only what our payment provider has confirmed. Never promise the bank's compensation for a delayed reversal: that is between the guest and their bank.

## 2. Refund status questions
### 2.1 Reporting progress
Report the refund's state exactly as our records show it: requested, approved, processing, completed or failed. Once our payment provider confirms a refund is completed, the guest's bank credits it within the timeline set per RBI turnaround-time rules. Say so when the refund is completed but the guest can't see it yet; don't quote a number of days as a promise.

## 3. Payment method
### 3.1 Original method only
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We never refund to a different account, card or UPI ID, even when the guest asks.
