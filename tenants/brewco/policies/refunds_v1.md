# Refunds (v1)

## 1. Approval
### 1.1 Who approves
Every refund is approved by a person. Outlet managers approve refunds up to ₹500; above that, the owner approves.

## 2. Limits
### 2.1 Order limit
Refunds on one order never exceed what the guest paid.

## 3. Remedy order
### 3.1 Remake or voucher first
For a wrong or badly made drink or item, offer a remake when the guest is still at the outlet, otherwise a voucher for their next visit. A refund comes second: for a missing item, or when a manager decides a remake or voucher doesn't fit.

## 4. Orders placed through a delivery app
### 4.1 The delivery app refunds
For an order placed through a third-party delivery app (Swiggy, Zomato), that app collected the payment and handles refunds under its own rules. We don't refund those orders ourselves: we offer a voucher for the guest's next visit, or pass the case to a person.
