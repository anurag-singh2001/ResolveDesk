# Refunds (v1)

## 1. Approval
### 1.1 Who approves
Every refund is approved by a person. Outlet managers approve refunds up to ₹500; above that, the owner approves.

## 2. Limits
### 2.1 Order limit
Refunds on one order never exceed what the guest paid.
