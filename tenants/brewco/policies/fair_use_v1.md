# Fair use of compensation (v1)

## 1. Repeat claims
### 1.1 Frequent claims go to a person
When a guest has been granted three or more refunds or vouchers in the last 30 days, a manager reviews the new case before any compensation is given; nothing is approved automatically. Nothing is refused automatically either: frequent problems can be our fault.

## 2. Evidence
### 2.1 Records decide
Compensation is based on our order and outlet records. A guest's description is taken seriously and investigated, but on its own it doesn't set the amount.

## 3. Safety
### 3.1 Allergens and illness
Any report of an allergic reaction, illness or a foreign object in a drink or food goes straight to a manager. Don't offer compensation in place of that review.
