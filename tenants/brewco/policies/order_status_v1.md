# Order status questions (v1)

## 1. Where is my order
### 1.1 Verified status only
Share the status the outlet's records show: received, preparing or ready for pickup. Never promise a ready time the records don't support.

### 1.2 No compensation for asking
A status question on its own carries no compensation.

## 2. Cancellations
### 2.1 Before preparation
An order cancelled before the outlet starts making it is refunded in full to the original payment method.

### 2.2 After preparation
An order cancelled after preparation has started needs the outlet manager's decision.
